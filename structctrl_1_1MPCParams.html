◆ rho_u

double ctrl::MPCParams::rho_u = 0.1

Move suppression weight (Ru = rhou.I_{Nc.m}).

Bryson's method starting point: rhou approx = 1/Deltau_max^2, where Deltau_max is the largest acceptable incremental control move per sample. Increasing rhou softens actuator activity at the cost of slower setpoint response and reduced disturbance rejection.

◆ rho_y

double ctrl::MPCParams::rho_y = 1.0

Output tracking weight (Qy = rhoy.I_{Np.p}).

Bryson's method starting point: rhoy approx = 1/e_max^2, where e_max is the largest acceptable peak tracking error. Only the ratio rhoy/rhou drives the response speed - scale both together to adjust absolute weights without changing the optimum. Larger rhoy/rhou -> tighter tracking, larger control moves and more noise sensitivity.
