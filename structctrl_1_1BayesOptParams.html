GaussianProcess::Params ctrl::BayesOptParams::gp

GP surrogate hyperparameters.

The optimizer normalises inputs to [0,1]^n before passing to the GP. A length scale of ~0.3 in normalised space is a reasonable default for parameter spaces with typical smoothness.
