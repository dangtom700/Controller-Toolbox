5// Discrete-time Extended Kalman Filter (EKF).
6//
7// Handles nonlinear systems:
8// x[k+1] = f(x[k], u[k]) + w[k], w ~ N(0, Q_noise)
9// y[k] = h(x[k], u[k]) + v[k], v ~ N(0, R_noise)
10//
11// NOISE MODEL ASSUMPTION: process and measurement noise are ADDITIVE (as above).
12// For multiplicative or state-dependent noise (e.g. gyroscope scale-factor noise,
13// Poisson photon counts), the EKF must be reformulated with augmented state and
14// noise channels. That augmented form is NOT implemented here.
15//
16// EKF linearises around the current estimate at each step:
17// F[k] = df/dx |_(x^[k|k], u[k]) - state Jacobian (n*n)
18// H[k] = dh/dx |_(x^[k+1|k], u[k]) - observation Jacobian (p*n)
19//
20// Predict:
21// x^[k+1|k] = f(x^[k|k], u[k])
22// P[k+1|k] = F[k].P[k|k].F[k]' + Q
23//
24// Update (Joseph form for numerical stability):
25// S = H[k].P[k+1|k].H[k]' + R
26// K = P[k+1|k].H[k]'.S^-1
27// x^[k+1|k+1] = x^[k+1|k] + K.(y - h(x^[k+1|k], u[k]))
28// P[k+1|k+1] = (I - K.H).P.(I - K.H)' + K.R.K'
29//
30// Jacobians can be analytical (preferred) or numerical via numericalJacobian().
31//
32// Ref: Jazwinski "Stochastic Processes and Filtering Theory" (1970);
33// Bar-Shalom et al. "Estimation with Applications to Tracking" (2001).
