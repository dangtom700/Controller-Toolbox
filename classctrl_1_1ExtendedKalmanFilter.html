Central-difference numerical Jacobian with state-scaled step size.

Per-element step: h_i = eps_scale . max(|x_i|, 1). This avoids near-zero absolute steps for small-magnitude states and cancellation errors for large-magnitude states. For heterogeneous state vectors (e.g., [position_m, velocity_m/s, angle_rad]) the fixed-epsilon approach degrades significantly; this formulation is robust across scales.

Use when analytical Jacobians are unavailable or too complex to maintain. Bind u as a constant to obtain df/dx or dh/dx:

auto Fjac = [&](const Eigen::VectorXd& x, const Eigen::VectorXd& u) {
return ExtendedKalmanFilter::numericalJacobian(
[&](const Eigen::VectorXd& xx){ return f(xx, u); }, x);
};
