Detailed Description

Index-1 semi-explicit Differential-Algebraic Equation system.

Represents plants of the form:

x1' = f(x1, x2, u) (n_diff differential states)
0 = g(x1, x2, u) (n_alg algebraic states)
y = h(x1, x2, u) (output map)

Use dae2ode() to obtain a discrete-time step function, consistentInit() to find consistent initial conditions, and the c2d() overload to obtain a linearised discrete-time StateSpace for controller design (MPC, LQR, LQG).

Note
Only SISO (scalar u) is supported at the DAE level. MIMO extensions can be layered on top by the user.
