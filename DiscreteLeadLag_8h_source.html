4// Discrete Lead-Lag / Lead / Lag Compensator.
5//
6// Continuous form: C(s) = K . (s + z_c) / (s + p_c)
7// Lead: p_c > z_c > 0 -> adds positive phase, improves phase margin / speed
8// Lag: 0 < p_c < z_c -> adds gain at low freq, improves steady-state accuracy
9//
10// Discretised via Tustin (bilinear) substitution s = 2(z-1)/(Ts(z+1)):
11// C(z) = b0 + b1.z^-^1
12// ------------ (first-order IIR)
13// 1 + a1.z^-^1
14//
15// b0 = K.(2/Ts + z_c) / (2/Ts + p_c)
16// b1 = K.(z_c - 2/Ts) / (2/Ts + p_c)
17// a1 = (p_c - 2/Ts) / (2/Ts + p_c)
18//
19// Difference equation: y[k] = b0.u[k] + b1.u[k-1] - a1.y[k-1]
20//
21// Typical use: placed in series with a PID or as a standalone compensator
22// in the forward or feedback path.
23//
24// Ref: Franklin, Powell & Emami-Naeini "Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems";
25// MATLAB c2d() with 'tustin' method.
