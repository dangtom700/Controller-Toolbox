◆ N_samples

int ctrl::ScenarioMPCParams::N_samples = 30

Number of noise scenarios sampled per control step.

Larger N_samples reduces variance in the cost estimate at the expense of computation. N_samples = 1 gives maximum variance; N_samples ~ 20-50 is a practical compromise for most applications.

Eigen::MatrixXd ctrl::ScenarioMPCParams::Sigma_w

Process noise covariance matrix (n x n).

At each step and each horizon sample, w[k] ~ N(0, Sigma_w). Zero Sigma_w reduces ScenarioMPC to deterministic MPC.
