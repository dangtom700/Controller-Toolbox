◆ b0

double ctrl::ADRCParams::b0 = 1.0

Approximate plant input gain b₀.

The control law u = (u₀ − z₃)/b₀ cancels the estimated total disturbance z₃; if b₀ is badly wrong, cancellation degrades and the ESO may diverge.

Robustness: ADRC tolerates b₀ uncertainty within roughly a factor of 3 for ω₀ ≥ 5·ωc (Gao 2003, §IV). Beyond that, disturbance cancellation degrades.

Practical starting estimates:

  • Motors: b₀ = Km / J (torque constant / inertia)
  • Integrating plants: b₀ = DC_gain / Ts²
  • Unknown plants: b₀ = 1/τ where τ is the open-loop time constant

Verify by running open-loop: the step response slope at t = 0⁺ ≈ b₀·u.
