Detailed Description

Smith Predictor wrapper for any IController inner loop.

Three overloaded constructors support integer-only dead time, explicit fractional Pade filters, and an automatic whole-dead-time constructor that splits theta = floor(theta/Ts).Ts + theta_frac.
