Detailed Description

Traits for DiscreteHinf — synthesis via DGKF bisection, not classical auto-tuners.

Performance weights W1/W2/W3 encode the design objectives. Use MixedSensitivity::build() and DiscreteHinf::solve() to synthesise the controller.
