Parameters
ff_modelDiscrete-time StateSpace G_ff(z) (single-input, single-output or MIMO if the plant is MIMO).
Note
G_ff must have Ts > 0 (already discretised). Use c2d() to convert a continuous-time inverse-plant model before passing it here.
