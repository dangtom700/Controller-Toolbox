Detailed Description

Discrete 2nd-order Sliding Mode Controller (super-twisting algorithm).

Eliminates chattering without a boundary layer while retaining finite-time convergence and robustness to matched disturbances bounded by a known Lipschitz constant.

Continuous-time super-twisting law (Levant 1993):

u[k] = v[k] - K1.|s|^{1/2}.sign(s)
v.[k] = -K2.sign(s)

Discrete approximation (Euler integration of v):

s[k] = c_e.e[k] + c_de.(e[k] - e[k-1])
u[k] = v[k] - K1.|s[k]|^{1/2}.sign(s[k])
v[k+1] = v[k] - K2.Ts.sign(s[k])

Gain selection (Moreno & Osorio 2008): K1 > 0 and K2 > K1^2/4 is sufficient for finite-time convergence in continuous time. In discrete time, keep K2.Ts ≪ K1 to limit discretisation error.
