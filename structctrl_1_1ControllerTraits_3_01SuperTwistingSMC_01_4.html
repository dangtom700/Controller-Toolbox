Detailed Description

Traits for SuperTwistingSMC — same design philosophy as DiscreteSMC.

Gains K1/K2 are derived from Moreno-Osorio Lyapunov conditions (K2 > K1²/4). No auto-tuner support; tune by Lyapunov analysis or empirical gain sweep.
